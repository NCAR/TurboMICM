## The entry

- **Generator:** `tools/specialize.py`, 337 lines of Python, beside Tier 1's
  generic constructor generator `tools/configure.py` (228 lines), reading the
  same normalized MICM configuration. The generic library (`src/`,
  `include/`) is unchanged from Tier 1.
- **What it specializes:** species order and every index, stoichiometry and
  yields, each rate formula with its fixed parameters, the negative Jacobian's
  2,381 filled entries, the Mozart LU plan's 8,439 operations, and the
  triangular solves become straight-line C; the Rosenbrock stages call the
  kernels directly. Loops run across the cells of a 32-cell group with a
  constant stride (`#pragma omp simd`), with a runtime trip count so the
  compiler does not unroll the whole group, and four operations fused per loop
  (its sweep's best of LU, block, fusion, and tile settings, all kept as make
  parameters).
- **What stays data:** temperature, pressure, air density, concentrations,
  every custom rate parameter (photolysis included), the step length, and the
  tolerances.
- **Generated C for TS1:** `build/generated/ts1_specialized_kernels.c`
  (111,167 lines) and `ts1_specialized.c` (3,032 lines), 114,209 lines and
  2.86 MB, regenerated by `make bench`; the report's count also includes Tier
  1's generated constructor `ts1.c` (1,543 lines).

- **TS1 gates:** the largest relative difference from MICM's final state over
  the 2,110,000 values, against a limit of 1e-10. The specialized solver takes
  MICM's 281,700 internal steps on both inputs.
- **The generator's tests:** TS1, MICM's three tutorial mechanisms (Robertson,
  user-defined rates, no user-defined rates), a mechanism with every rate
  family, and one with no reactions: rates, forcing, Jacobian, LU, solve, and
  adaptive trajectories against the generic library to 1e-10.
- **Day-night:** agreement with MICM at relative 1e-6, absolute 1e-15, 32-cell
  groups, 14.05 to 14.45 digits overall and at least 13.43 for every species,
  on the five disclosed and five held-out scenarios. On every scenario it
  takes exactly MICM's internal steps, and its accuracy against the reference
  solutions (4.73 to 5.40 digits) is MICM's, to the rounding of the scores. MICM's own clang and gcc-15
  builds agree to 14.07 to 14.37 digits on the disclosed five.
- **Audit:** flags `-std=c11`, warnings, `-O3 -DNDEBUG`, include paths,
  OpenMP, and defines of the group size and kernel width; every program links
  only libomp and libSystem; no banned construct and no process call in the 44
  C files, generated included.

## Findings

- **astra-max passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** and its
  specialized solver reproduces MICM's step sequence exactly on all ten
  day-night scenarios, agreeing to 14 digits, as MICM's own two compilers do.
- **Specialization gains 1.23 times over its own generic solver** at 18
  threads, which itself runs as in Tier 1 (the Tier 1 and Tier 2 generic
  binaries are the same size and time within 0.2 %). The entry's own
  measurement, 1.26 times (3.063 s against 3.872 s, its RESULTS.md), agrees.
- **Against MICM:** 3.89 times its default and 1.44 times its fastest
  configuration on this machine.
- **The generator is small:** 337 lines of Python write 114,209 lines of C
  for TS1, and run on five other mechanisms in its tests.
- **The run took 1 h 7 min and 182,784 tokens**, one turn, no compaction.
