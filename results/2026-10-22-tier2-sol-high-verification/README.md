## The entry

- **Generator:** `tools/specialize.py` (350 lines of Python) with
  `tools/compact_linear.py` (129), `tools/compact_chemistry.py` (82), and
  `tools/evaluate.py` (97), on Tier 1's configuration reader. The Rosenbrock
  algorithm moved into a template shared by the generic and the generated
  solvers (`src/rosenbrock_impl.h`).
- **What it specializes:** species ordering, the sparse coordinates and fill,
  the derivative jobs, the reaction arithmetic, yields, rate formulas with
  fixed coefficients, and the triangular-solve rows. The chemistry and the
  Jacobian are emitted as direct code; the LU factorization is emitted as
  immutable compact operation tables that short shared SIMD kernels run, four
  lower updates sharing each load of the upper factor, with reciprocal
  pivots. The three stages' vector passes are fused, each element in its
  order; error terms are computed with SIMD and summed in the original strict
  order.
- **Its experiments** (SPECIALIZATION.md): fully unrolled LU was slower than
  the compact tables, and cell tiles slower still on this machine.
- **A bug its final review caught:** negative fixed coefficients could be
  emitted as an invalid C token; it fixed the emission and added test cases.
- **What stays data:** temperature, pressure, air density, concentrations,
  every custom rate parameter, the step length, and the tolerances.
- **Generated C for TS1:** `build/ts1_specialized.c`, 26,300 lines and
  0.99 MB, the smallest so far, regenerated by `make bench`; the report's count
  also includes Tier 1's `build/ts1.c` (1,651 lines).

## Findings

- **sol-high passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** with MICM's
  step sequence on every day-night scenario.
- **Specialization gains 1.37 times over its own generic solver** at 18
  threads, the second-largest gain so far, after astra-xhigh's 1.51, with the
  least generated C (26,300 lines), since its LU is a table run by shared
  kernels. Its own report, 1.44 times its Tier 1 benchmark, is a little higher.
- **Against MICM:** 4.19 times its default and 1.53 times its fastest
  configuration; fourth of the six entries so far, 1.4 % behind sol-xhigh,
  within the session's spread.
- **The run took 1 h 15 min and 451,395 tokens**, one turn, no compaction,
  and its final review caught and fixed a generator bug (negative fixed
  coefficients).
