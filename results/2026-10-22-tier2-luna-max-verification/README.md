## The entry

- **Generator:** its Tier 1 table generator, `tools/generate_c.py`, extended by
  306 lines to emit, for a mechanism, fixed rate, chemistry and Jacobian,
  sparse LU, and triangular-solve kernels; the generic solver
  (`src/solver.c`) takes them as optional callbacks and falls back to its own
  path when constraints change the sparse pattern.
- **What it specializes:** species and reaction indices, multiplicities,
  yields, rate parameters, the species order (MICM's diagonal Markowitz), the
  sparsity and fill, and every LU and triangular-solve operation, as
  individual C statements, with the generic code's `fma` update order. Its
  ablation switches (`--without-*-kernel`) show each of the chemistry, LU, and
  solve kernels contributing.
- **Grouping:** its benchmark keeps MICM's default grouping, one group of
  cells per thread, as its Tier 1 benchmark did, so it does the same solver
  work as MICM's default (16,200 internal steps at 18 threads); its day-night
  runs use 32-cell groups through `--group-size 32`.
- **What stays data:** temperature, pressure, air density, concentrations,
  custom rate parameters, the step length, and the tolerances.
- **Generated C for TS1:** `build/ts1_model_specialized.c`, 28,434 lines and
  1.45 MB, regenerated by `make bench`; the report's count also includes Tier
  1's generated constructor `build/ts1_model.c` (951 lines).

## Findings

- **luna-max passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** with MICM's
  step sequence on every day-night scenario.
- **Specialization gains 2.84 times over its own generic solver,** the
  largest gain of any entry so far, because its generic solver is the slowest
  (15.3 s, slower than MICM's default as in Tier 1). Its own report, 2.84 times
  (5,307 against 15,063 ms), agrees.
- **Against MICM:** 2.17 times its default, with the same grouping, but 0.79
  times its fastest configuration, the first entry not to beat MICM's fastest.
  The six earlier entries' specialized solvers ran 1.8 to 2.7 times faster in
  the same session.
- **Its Tier 2 generic solver is 3.9 % slower than its Tier 1 build,**
  probably the cost of the new dispatch to optional generated kernels; the
  ranges overlap.
- **The run took 29 min 26 s and 466,476 tokens**, one turn with one context
  compaction: the shortest Tier 2 run.
