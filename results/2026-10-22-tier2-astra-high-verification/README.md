## The entry

- **Generator:** `tools/specialize.py`, 406 lines of Python, beside Tier 1's
  generic constructor generator (`tools/configure.py`). The Rosenbrock step
  moved into a 109-line C template, `src/rosenbrock_step.inc`, that the
  generic and the generated solvers share.
- **What it specializes:** species indices, reactant occurrences, yields, and
  rate formulas with literal coefficients; the generic library's Markowitz
  order and the LU fill (2,381 entries) computed in Python; forcing,
  Jacobian, LU, and both triangular sweeps as straight-line C with literal
  offsets; a compile-time stride of 32 cells (other group widths fall back to
  the generic kernels); the final-state and error loops of the three stages
  fused, each element's order kept.
- **An exact rate cache:** the generated rate update keeps a copy of each
  state's conditions and custom parameters and skips the rate constants when
  they are byte for byte unchanged (`GEN_OPTIONS=--no-cache` turns it off).
  astra-high's Tier 1 benchmark, unlike the others and MICM's driver,
  recomputes every rate constant in each timed step, which its Tier 2
  benchmarks keep; in the benchmark's constant conditions the cache skips that
  work. Timed without it (below), it accounts for 2 % of the gain.
- **What stays data:** temperature, pressure, air density, concentrations,
  every custom rate parameter, the step length, and the tolerances.
- **Generated C for TS1:** `build/specialized/ts1.c`, 44,974 lines and 2.05 MB,
  regenerated by `make bench`; the report's count also includes Tier 1's
  generated constructor `build/ts1.c` (6,459 lines).

## Findings

- **astra-high passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** with MICM's
  step sequence on every day-night scenario.
- **Specialization gains 1.25 times over its own generic solver,** 1.22 times
  without the rate cache: the cache, which removes the per-step rate update
  its benchmark times and the others' do not, is worth 2 %. Its own report,
  1.25 times its Tier 1 benchmark, agrees.
- **Against MICM:** 3.54 times its default and 1.32 times its fastest
  configuration.
- **Against the other Astra entries, in the same session:** slower than
  astra-xhigh's specialized solver (2.05 s) and astra-max's (2.98 s).
- **The run took 1 h 7 min and 329,804 tokens**, one turn, no compaction.
