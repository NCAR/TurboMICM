## The entry

- **Generator:** its Tier 1 generator, `tools/generate.py`, extended by 524
  lines to emit, besides the mechanism descriptor, a specialized solver entry
  point; the specialized benchmark and scenario drivers are new code in
  `src/ts1_bench.c` (310 lines added).
- **What it specializes:** the reaction topology, indices, stoichiometry, and
  yields; each rate kind as its own formula with its fixed constants; the
  Jacobian's sparsity, a deterministic low-fill species order computed at
  generation time, and the exact Mozart factor updates and triangular solves;
  forcing and Jacobian contributions emitted per reaction. State is stored
  species-major across the cells of a group.
- **Grouping and timer:** its benchmark keeps MICM's default grouping, one
  group of cells per thread, and its Tier 1 timed region: one call per group
  covering all the timed steps (its Tier 1 benchmark also timed each group's
  setup in that call).
- **What stays data:** cell temperature, pressure, air density, user-defined
  parameters, the state, the tolerances, the step length, and the scenario's
  photolysis values.
- **Generated C for TS1:** `build/generated/ts1.c`, descriptor and specialized
  solver in one file, 48,068 lines and 2.40 MB, regenerated by the build; it
  takes about a minute to compile with either compiler.

## Findings

- **luna-xhigh passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** with MICM's
  step sequence on every day-night scenario.
- **Specialization gains 1.07 times over its own generic solver,** the least
  of the eight entries so far; its own report, 1.07 times (9,975 against 10,721
  ms), agrees.
- **Against MICM:** 1.16 times its default, with the same grouping, and 0.43
  times its fastest configuration; the slowest of the eight specialized
  solvers, five times slower than astra-xhigh's in the same session.
- **Its time barely shrinks with threads:** 48 s on one thread and 10 s on 18,
  by its own measurements, a scaling of 4.8; its Tier 1 entry scaled 2.79
  times from 1 to 10 threads on the M5, the least of any program then. Its
  timed call per group still does the group's setup, as in Tier 1.
- **The run took 35 min 44 s and 571,245 tokens**, one turn with one context
  compaction.
