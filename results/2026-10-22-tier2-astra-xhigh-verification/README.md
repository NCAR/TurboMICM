## The entry

- **Generator:** its Tier 1 code already generated TS1's chemistry kernels
  (`tools/generate.py`, `tools/configure.py`, writing `generated/ts1.c`, 16,341
  lines). Tier 2 adds 492 lines of Python in five modules, `tools/specialize.py`
  (191), `tiled.py` (91), `specialize_rates.py` (79), `symbolic.py` (71), and
  `split_kernels.py` (60), and a 112-line C template of the three-stage solver,
  `tools/ros3.c.in`.
- **What it specializes:** the symbolic planner fixes the species order
  (MICM's Markowitz order), the Jacobian's 2,381 filled entries, and the LU
  elimination, all emitted as straight-line C in loops across cells. The
  defaults, from its measurements: Doolittle LU, accumulating each entry in a
  local variable, with reciprocal pivots stored in the packed factors; a
  private workspace of four-cell tiles inside each 32-cell group, which keeps
  the group's single step controller and excludes padding lanes from the error
  norm, summed in the original order; batches of eight chemistry and algebra
  jobs per loop; functions of about 256 lines, called through private volatile
  function pointers so that the compiler cannot merge them (ISO C, no
  attribute or flag).
- **What stays data:** temperature, pressure, air density, concentrations,
  every user-defined, photolysis, emission, loss, and surface parameter, the
  step length, and the tolerances.
- **The generic library:** unchanged but for one line of `src/state.c`; the
  benchmark's input and output moved to `src/bench_io.h`, and the day-night
  driver is new (`src/scenario.c`).
- **Generated C for TS1:** `generated/ts1_specialized.c`, 75,172 lines and
  2.61 MB, regenerated by `make bench`; the report's count also includes Tier
  1's `generated/ts1.c`.

## Findings

- **astra-xhigh passes all of Tier 2's gate with both compilers,** with MICM's
  step sequence on every day-night scenario.
- **Specialization gains 1.51 times over its own generic solver** at 18
  threads, and its specialized solver is 1.45 times as fast as astra-max's.
  Its own report, 1.55 times its Tier 1 benchmark (2.012 s), agrees.
- **The gain came from layout and the LU schedule, not unrolling:** its first
  fully unrolled version was slower than its Tier 1 code (3.16 against 3.00
  s, its SPECIALIZATION.md); tiles, reciprocal pivots, and Doolittle
  accumulation brought it to 2.0 s.
- **Against MICM:** 5.70 times its default and 2.11 times its fastest
  configuration.
- **The run took 1 h 14 min and 369,502 tokens**, one turn, no compaction.
